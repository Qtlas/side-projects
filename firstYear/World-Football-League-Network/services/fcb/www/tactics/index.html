Primary formation: 4-3-3

The side plays mainly in a 4-3-3, balancing defensive stability with attacking width.

In possession: build from the back, quick ball circulation, wingers stretching the pitch to create space.

Out of possession: organised pressing on loss, fast recovery into a compact block, lateral cover assured.
